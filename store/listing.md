**Full description** (max 4000):
```
Plan your Margazhi (December) music season in Chennai.

Marghazhi Kutcheri Planner brings together the Carnatic music concerts that sabhas and artists have published for the 2026–27 season, so you can find what to hear and plan your day around it.

FIND CONCERTS
• Search by artist — including accompanists on violin, mridangam, ghatam, kanjira and more
• Filter by date (pick one or several days on the calendar), concert type (vocal, violin, flute, veena, nagaswaram…), area and sabha
• See timings, venue, entry (free or ticketed) and the full line-up for each kutcheri

PLAN YOUR DAY
• Star concerts to add them to My plan
• See clashes and tight gaps between concerts on the same day
• Get rough travel times between venues and open the whole day's route in Google Maps

SABHAS
• Browse 64 sabhas and organisers, see which have published their 2026–27 schedule, and jump to their concerts or website

HELP COMPLETE THE LIST
• Spotted a concert that's missing? Send it with a link or a photo of the notice
• Organisers can send their sabha's whole schedule

ALWAYS UP TO DATE, WORKS OFFLINE
• The list is refreshed every morning from organisers' published schedules and artists' own websites
• The latest list is saved on your phone, so it works without a connection at the venue

No sign-in and no ads. Your plan stays on your phone; anonymous visit counts (no cookies) help us see how the planner is used.

Schedules can change — please confirm with the organiser before you travel. This app is an independent listing and is not affiliated with any sabha or artist.
```
