# Privacy policy — Marghazhi Kutcheri Planner (Android app)

_Last updated: 12 October 2026 (GoatCounter usage counts; optional report forms)_

Marghazhi Kutcheri Planner lists publicly announced Carnatic music concerts in Chennai for the 2026–27 Margazhi (December) season.

**What the app collects:** only anonymous usage counts, through GoatCounter (https://www.goatcounter.com), so we can see how many people use the planner and from which countries. Each count records the screen or tab opened (and when a concert is added to or removed from My plan), the device type, screen size and language, and the country and region, which GoatCounter works out from the network address and then discards. GoatCounter uses no cookies and stores no IP address or identifier; it tells unique visits apart with a daily-changing anonymous hash. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or precise location are collected, and the app has no accounts, sign-in or advertising. The statistics are not sold or shared. GoatCounter's own privacy policy: https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy.

**What you choose to send:** the app has two optional forms — "Add a missing concert" and, for organisers, "Send your schedule". What you type or attach there (concert details, a link, a photo or PDF of a notice or schedule, and any name, email address or phone number you add) is sent through Netlify Forms and emailed to the planner's owner, only so the concert can be checked and added. It is not published, sold or shared, and is deleted once it has been dealt with. The missing-concert form does not require a name or contact details; the organiser form asks for a contact person and an email address or phone number so we can confirm the schedule. Netlify's privacy policy: https://www.netlify.com/privacy/.

**What stays on your phone:** your starred concerts ("My plan"), your filter choices, and a saved copy of the concert list, so the app works offline. This information is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

**Network use:** when the phone is online, the app sends the anonymous usage counts above to GoatCounter and downloads the latest public concert list from https://kutcheri-dec.netlify.app (hosted by Netlify) or, if that is unavailable, from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). These are ordinary file downloads; no personal information is included. As with any website, those hosting providers may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) under their own privacy policies.

**Links:** tapping a map, sabha or artist link opens it in your browser or Google Maps, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

**Children:** the app is not directed at children.

**Contact:** questions about this policy can be raised as an issue at https://github.com/sunithi2000-lab/kutcheri-android/issues.
