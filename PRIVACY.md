# Privacy policy — Marghazhi Kutcheri Planner (Android app)

_Last updated: 12 October 2026 (added Google Analytics)_

Marghazhi Kutcheri Planner lists publicly announced Carnatic music concerts in Chennai for the 2026–27 Margazhi (December) season.

**What the app collects:** only anonymous usage statistics, through Google Analytics, so we can see how many people use the planner and from which countries. It records which screens and tabs are opened, when a concert is added to or removed from My plan, the device type and operating system, and an approximate location (country, region and city) that Google works out from the network address. Google Analytics stores a random identifier in the app to tell visits apart. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or precise location are collected, and the app has no accounts, sign-in or advertising.

Google Analytics is provided by Google as a service provider; see https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://business.safety.google/privacy/. Data is kept for Google Analytics' standard retention period (2 months for detailed reports) and is not sold or shared with anyone else.

**What stays on your phone:** your starred concerts ("My plan"), your filter choices, and a saved copy of the concert list, so the app works offline. This information is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

**Network use:** when the phone is online, the app sends the usage statistics above to Google Analytics and downloads the latest public concert list from https://kutcheri-dec.netlify.app (hosted by Netlify) or, if that is unavailable, from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). These are ordinary file downloads; no personal information is included. As with any website, those hosting providers may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) under their own privacy policies.

**Links:** tapping a map, sabha or artist link opens it in your browser or Google Maps, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

**Children:** the app is not directed at children.

**Contact:** questions about this policy can be raised as an issue at https://github.com/sunithi2000-lab/kutcheri-android/issues.
