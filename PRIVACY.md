# Privacy policy — Marghazhi Kutcheri Planner (Android app)

_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

Marghazhi Kutcheri Planner lists publicly announced Carnatic music concerts in Chennai for the 2026–27 Margazhi (December) season.

**What the app collects:** nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

**What stays on your phone:** your starred concerts ("My plan"), your filter choices, and a saved copy of the concert list, so the app works offline. This information is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

**Network use:** when the phone is online, the app downloads the latest public concert list from https://kutcheri-dec.netlify.app (hosted by Netlify) or, if that is unavailable, from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). These are ordinary file downloads; no personal information is included. As with any website, those hosting providers may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) under their own privacy policies.

**Links:** tapping a map, sabha or artist link opens it in your browser or Google Maps, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

**Children:** the app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone.

**Contact:** questions about this policy can be raised as an issue at https://github.com/sunithi2000-lab/kutcheri-android/issues.
